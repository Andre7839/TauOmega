Forward model equations
Tb_p = Tc·(1−ω)·(1−γ_p)·(1+r_p·γ_p) + Ts·(1−r_p)·γ_p + Tsky·r_p·γ_p²
γ_p = exp(−τ/cosθ)   |   e_p = 1 − r_p   |   p ∈ {V, H}
r_p_rough = [(1−Q)·r_p_smooth + Q·r_q_smooth]·exp(−h_r·cosⁿθ)
h_r = (2k·h_rms)²   k = 2πf/c   f = 1.4 GHz
ε(SMC, Ts) via Wang & Schmugge (1980) → Fresnel → r_V, r_H
Inversion: Nelder-Mead simplex minimising (TbV_obs−TbV_sim)² + (TbH_obs−TbH_sim)²
Model refs: Mo & Schmugge (1987) · Wigneron et al. (2007) · Wang & Schmugge (1980) · Wang (1983) Q-h roughness
Valid range: SMC 0.005–0.60 m³/m³ · τ 0–3 · θ 0–60° · frequency L-band 1.4 GHz
